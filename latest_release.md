## ncCAM 0.1.0 (beta)

Turn a STEP part into G-code right inside ncSender, using the tools in your Tool Library.

- **Open your part:** drop a `.step`/`.stp` file. ncCAM finds the pockets, holes, steps and outline, and offers only the sides that can be cut from the top.
- **Stock & Zero:** pick the material, size the stock, tap where zero is on a 3D view, and leave tabs to hold the part.
- **Operations:** each operation gets an end mill or drill from your Tool Library. Change the tool, switch operations off, and pick Fast, Balanced or Smooth.
- **Preview & Load:** watch the cut play through as material is removed, with checks for fast moves into the material and machine travel, then load it or save it to the File Manager.

2.5D parts only (flat floors and straight walls, one setup from the top). Chamfers and fillets are left square for now. Feedback welcome.
